Pro Tips

Elevate Your Game

01
Master Your Grip

A relaxed, consistent grip pressure of about 5 out of 10 allows maximum club head speed and better feel through impact.

02
Read the Green

Walk around the putt to observe slope from multiple angles. The last few feet before the hole matter most — that's where the ball slows and breaks.

03
Play the Wind

Toss a little grass in the air or feel the breeze on your skin. Club up into the wind, club down with it, and aim for the middle of the green.

04
Course Management

Your best score comes from smart decisions, not hero shots. Lay up, avoid hazards, and give yourself easy angles for approach shots.

05
Commit to Every Shot

Doubt is a swing killer. Once you've chosen your club and target, commit fully. Indecision creates tension that ruins timing and tempo.

06
Short Game = Low Scores

80% of shots occur within 100 yards of the hole. Spend at least half your practice time chipping and putting — that's where strokes are saved.
